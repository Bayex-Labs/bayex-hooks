Why LP-ing Doesn't Work Today

Prediction market LPs face a double disadvantage that doesn't exist in regular DeFi pools.

Problem 1: Arb Extraction

When external odds shift (news, orderbook movement), arbitrageurs trade against stale AMM prices and extract value from LPs before the pool adjusts.

This is Loss-Versus-Rebalancing (LVR) — and it's amplified in prediction markets because prices move sharply around events.

LPs subsidize arb bots that contribute nothing beyond bridging a latency gap.

Problem 2: Worthless Fees

Conditional tokens resolve to either $1 or $0. Standard AMMs collect fees in both tokens — meaning LPs accumulate fees in a token that might become worthless.

An LP who earned 5% in YES-token fees earns nothing if YES resolves to zero.

Even profitable LPs can end up with zero real returns at resolution.

Together, these problems make LP-ing a losing proposition — so traders keep their capital idle instead.

Bayex: Two Solutions in One Hook

A Uniswap v4 hook that makes prediction market LP-ing safe and profitable.

How It Works

Each LP configures a fee denomination split (default: 100% USDC). The hook bypasses v4's built-in fee mechanism and collects fees directly using both delta return hooks.

Aggregate Split

The weighted average of all LP preferences determines the actual USDC/token fee ratio that swappers pay.

Fair Distribution

Fee-per-liquidity accumulators with checkpointing ensure each LP earns proportional to their liquidity and split preference.

Registration

LP address passed via hookData. Default: 100% USDC fees. Only trusted routers can register LPs.

Fee Split Config

LP calls configureFeeSplit() — e.g. 70/30 USDC/token. Existing fees snapshotted before change.

Accumulation

Separate USDC and token accumulators track each LP's share weighted by denomination preference.

Claims & Exit

LP calls claimFees() anytime. Accrued fees preserved even after removing liquidity.
